# Gap Map

A gap must be expressed as a **mismatch or unresolved dependency**, not merely "few papers".

| Gap ID | Observed mismatch | Evidence | Why existing approaches do not resolve it | What would falsify the gap? | Research opportunity | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Candidate gap patterns to test, not assume

- models consume structure but do not expose interaction-state change as a computational object;
- models achieve good random-split performance but fail on meaningful family/complex/OOD transfer;
- local mutation effects are predicted without explicitly modeling changed interface relations;
- interface-centric methods exist, but their benefit may come from a simpler information bottleneck rather than dynamic updating.
